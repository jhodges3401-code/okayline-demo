FAQ

Is Okayline a one-time payment?

Yes. $99 once for the Pro template. Hosting on GitHub Pages is free for public repos.

Is there a free version?

Yes. Okayline Lite is MIT-licensed on GitHub.

Is Okayline affiliated with Better Stack?

No. It's an independent product built from scratch.
